### 4. SYNTHESIS

**Recurring properties he praises:**

- Clean, structured information architecture (referencing Lighthouse).
- The core conceptual proposition of building an "encoded layer" into the client's organization.
- The visual design language of the "arc" from their homepage (wants it adapted cleanly for proposals).
- High-quality, visually appealing diagrams (like the plugin diagram).

**Recurring properties he rejects:**

- **Poor use of space/density:** He repeatedly points out areas that are either too crammed (bullet points) or have too much wasted white space (The Approach diagram).
- **Off-brand visual elements:** He strongly dislikes elements that look like "children's drawings," doodle arrows, and rounded corners, stating they do not fit the brand system.
- **AI-generated typography patterns:** He hates the trend of breaking a sentence apart and making one random word/phrase a massive font (e.g., "0 FRAMES", "Weeks", "Op alles").
- **Repetitive layouts:** He is annoyed by the repeated use of a "three block structure" across different sections.
- **Irrelevant metadata:** He explicitly rejects tags like "Made in Claude" and dates, emphasizing that the client name is what matters.

**What the page must say or show that it does not yet:**

- **A cohesive narrative thread:** The page currently feels like disjointed sections. It must explicitly explain how the four workstreams (Creative production, ops, review, strategy) plug into the "encoded layer," and how that layer is built through adoption and automation.
- **Smooth transitions:** The jump into the Loop case study must make sense from a storytelling and grammatical perspective, rather than just appearing out of nowhere.
- **Culmination of proof:** The "What it returned at Loop" section must feel like the ultimate payoff/culmination of the case study, not just an extra card tacked onto the end.
- **Meaningful copy:** Every piece of text must make total sense to the reader (e.g., fixing confusing lines like "not reviewed yet") and offer a real narrative proposition (e.g., the Plugin section).
- **Proper data visualization:** Where data exists (like the marketing drive), it must show an actual visual map or cluster, not just text.
