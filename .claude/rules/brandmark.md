# Rule: Brandmark / sigil choreography

Edits here move a brandmark actor across fixed, projected, or in-canvas renderers. Small measurement mistakes show up as hero flashes, between-section float, practice snaps, or a visible SVG-to-particle size jump.

**Read first**

- [ADR-010: Brandmark choreography](../sentinel/decisions/010-brandmark-choreography.md)
- [ADR-023: Corridor Brandmark Physics Core](../sentinel/decisions/023-corridor-brandmark-physics-core.md)
- `.claude/skills/brandmark-choreography/SKILL.md` (checklist + Playwright recipe)
- Related stack: [ADR-008](../sentinel/decisions/008-landing-v7-background-layers.md) (layers)

**Process:** [sentinel/MAINTENANCE.md](../sentinel/MAINTENANCE.md) - run Cycle A after changing triggers; prefer live rects and `onLeave` settlement over new CSS sticky hacks.

**Home-v2 corridor invariant (REWRITTEN 2026-06-25 hybrid):** the live corridor brandmark is **two painters with an invisible seam**, not one. `ProjectedBrandmarkActor` owns the visible mark at full opacity (instant `display: block`, never an opacity fade) while corridor `progress < BRANDMARK_CORE_HANDOFF_PROGRESS` (`= CORRIDOR_TIMELINE.brandmark.thoughtformHold`, currently `0.14` — pulled earlier than the old `0.30` substrate-wrap anchor to give the morph more runway). The instant `progress` crosses that threshold from below, the SVG cuts to `display: none` in the SAME frame that `BrandmarkPhysicsCoreActor` rasterises the SVG's live screen rect (via `rasterizeBrandmarkToWorldPositions` + `worldPositionsToLocal` + `sim.reseed`/`sim.setHomePositions`) into world positions that reproject to those exact CSS pixels — so the eye sees no swap because the particles ARE the SVG at frame N+1. The two painters share a `brandmarkScreenRectRef` (`components/landing/home-v2/brandmarkScreenRectRef.ts`) — the SVG writes its rect every paint frame; the physics-core actor reads it at the swap frame. From there the vertex shader interpolates each particle from its matched-pixel seed to its paired `aTarget3D` (the GLB volumetric wireframe sampled from `/models/brandmark/brandmark.glb` via `sampleBrandmark3D` — the SAME mesh the #services hologram uses) with per-particle staggered `flowT`, +Z recede peaking mid-flow, and low-amplitude XY drift. By `uDepth = 1` (at substrate wrap peak, `progress = 0.42`) every particle has settled exactly on its wireframe home inside the substrate sphere. Pitfalls: cross-fading SVG and particles together (any non-zero opacity overlap reads as the dissolve the user explicitly rejected); using opacity instead of `display: none` for the SVG cut; using a synthetic dome bulge as the morph endpoint instead of `sampleBrandmark3D.armHomes`; making the particles try to look exactly like the SVG at rest (perceptually impossible at corridor density — five revisions tried, all failed; that's why the SVG owns the rest now); lowering `ignite` to create a swirl. The sim stays assembled (`seedAtHome`, `ignite=1`); the render shader owns the wind-blown flat → wireframe interpolation. ADR-023 Invariants 13–14 are the load-bearing contract.

**Harmonization (2026-06-25 follow-up; color superseded by the unify-on-orange note below AND the 2026-07-06 parked-convergence note here):** the SETTLED in-sphere mark reads as a crisp, airy wireframe that matches #services so the corridor→Services transition is continuation, not a swap. A `wireCrisp = smoothstep(0.7, 1.0, uDepth)` factor (vertex→`vWireCrisp`) tightens the dots + stills twinkle + shrinks specks, plus a settled alpha trim; the crisp/still/shrink effects remain load-bearing. **Color contract (2026-07-06 "one holographic instrument" pass):** the `uColor → uLandedColor` body/accent lerp is gated by **`uCleanField` (= `recT`, the shrink-into-#services ramp), NOT `vWireCrisp`** — the flat rest, matched-pixel flight and in-sphere wireframe stay pure `TENSOR_GOLD` (handoff color-seamless, byte-identical pre-dive), and the mark gradients subtly to **`SERVICES_MARK_GOLD` (`#dcc176`, the ALPHA-COMPENSATED lift of the site `--gold` — feeding the literal `#caa554` still _read_ as the old orange because the mark is soft-falloff dots alpha-composited over the void, perceived ≈ one shade darker than fed)** only as it docks as the #services centerpiece, matching the plates/connectors/chips/armillary perceptually (the `DEFAULT_ORBITS` service rings are `SERVICES_GOLD` `#caa554` — solid lines take the literal value; particles take the compensated one). The same `recT` ramp also boosts parked density/weight (`cleanFieldKeepRef` 0.65→0.82, `cleanFieldDotScaleRef` 0.5→0.68 in `BrandmarkPhysicsCoreActor` — `CENTER_FIELD_*`/`CENTER_DOT_*` constants). Production #services is FRONTAL (`restTilt 0/0`) to match the head-on in-sphere mark — keep both frontal (the sphere's sword↔vertical-orbit alignment depends on a frontal mark). Pitfalls: ramping `wireCrisp` in before uDepth ~0.7 (flattens the fly-in); re-tilting one of {in-sphere mark, #services wireframe} without the other; re-gating the color lerp back to `vWireCrisp` (recolors the in-sphere mark mid-corridor).

**Never-warp + desaturate + align (2026-06-25 follow-up).** The PRESENTED brandmark shape (flat rest + landed wireframe) must never warp/wobble — only the wind-blown flight may move particles. So: the corridor sim is PINNED (`PINNED_CORRIDOR_FORCES` = flow/turbulence 0, returnStrength full) — the morph is vertex-shader-driven off the pinned home, so pinning doesn't touch the transition; the rasterizer guarantees ≥ count unique candidates (no seed-wrap duplicates → no doubled-dot hot spots); and `vCrisp` (high at flat rest AND landed) tightens + stills + flattens size-variance so the shape reads clean. `PRODUCTION_BLENDING` is `normal` (not additive): additive accumulation over the dense mark clipped to a vivid over-saturated gold, normal blending settles at the true muted unified gold (`#b08b42`, see unify note) and matches the normal-blend #services wireframe. `TARGET_HALF = 0.5` is the group-local half-extent the GLB sample normalizes to; the mark's WORLD size is then `BRANDMARK_SPHERE_FILL` (`sceneGeom.ts`, `0.95`) of the sphere's apparent radius so the wireframe FILLS the sphere — its outer extent reaches the visible edge — while the sword tip stays just inside (`1.0` put the sword on the dotted shell and it read as protruding; `0.85` was an over-correction that read as too small). Pitfalls: re-enabling sim flow/turbulence for the corridor (re-warps); reverting to additive (re-saturates); padding the seed/target buffers with zeros instead of wrapping (origin "photon" cluster); raising `BRANDMARK_SPHERE_FILL` toward 1.0 (sword pokes past the sphere again) or dropping it well below 0.9 (mark reads too small inside the sphere).

**Unification — one object into #services (2026-06-25 follow-up).** The corridor core IS the #services centerpiece; it no longer cross-dissolves into a SECOND brandmark in a separate canvas. Gated by `UNIFIED_SERVICES_ARMILLARY` (`components/landing/home-v2/unifiedServicesInstrument.ts`). The core does NOT fade out at the dive (`handoffFade` is now `docked ? 1 : servicesAmbient ? servicesAmbientLevel : 1` — full through the dock + ambient, fading only as `#continuum` nears). The #services orbit armillary is a CHILD of the core's group via `CorridorArmillary` (renders `HologramOrbits` + `DEFAULT_ORBITS` in the SAME corridor canvas, so orbits depth-interleave with the mark and inherit its billboard/drift/scale); a `pointerLookRef` wrapper tilts mark+orbits toward the cursor when parked. Scan anchors publish to `hologramConnectorStore` only once parked; `ServicesStage` writes `activeServiceId` to that store as the cross-tree bridge and stops mounting its own R3F canvas (`showServicesCanvas = useHologramCanvas && !UNIFIED_SERVICES_ARMILLARY`). The standalone `ServicesHologramScene`/`VolumetricBrandmarkArtifact` remain the lab harness. Color flows from `lib/home-v2/goldPalette.ts` (see unify note below for the live values). Pitfalls: reintroducing a separate #services canvas/crossfade (the dissolve the user rejected); ungating the armillary/anchors from `recT`/dissipate (orbits leak into the corridor proper); breaking the mobile/reduced-motion DOM+SVG fallback (it must stay — the corridor never docks there).

**Unify on one gold → darker orange + contain + de-pixelate (2026-06-25 follow-up; CURRENT color contract).** The corridor brandmark is **one continuous color** end-to-end — 2D rest SVG, matched-pixel flight, landed wireframe, orbits, and #services all read the same gold, with NO mid-journey color lerp (the `wireCrisp` color shift is a no-op; its crisp/still/shrink effects remain). The unified value was briefly a too-yellow "Tensor" (`#c2af4c`) and is now the **darker yellow-orange retrofuturistic glow** at the user's request. Live palette in `lib/home-v2/goldPalette.ts`: `TENSOR_GOLD` = `#b08b42` (body, rgb 176,139,66), `TENSOR_ACCENT` = `#dcc176` (accent), `SPHERE_GOLD` = `0xcaa554` (substrate-sphere gimbal + interior particles, a touch lighter). The `TENSOR_*` names are kept as stable semantic labels (not hue names) to avoid churning ~30 import sites; the retired `HANDOFF_*` constants are gone. The 2D rest SVG fill is set via the new `BrandmarkGlyph` `fill` prop (`ProjectedBrandmarkActor` passes `TENSOR_GOLD`); its drop-shadow glow + the mobile fallbacks (`ServicesBrandmarkField`, `CorridorSeamPixelField` `GOLD_RGB`) carry the hardcoded `176, 139, 66`. The global site brand gold `--gold` (`#caa554`, `app/styles/variables.css`) is unchanged — this is scoped to the corridor brandmark + sphere. **Size/containment:** `BRANDMARK_SPHERE_FILL` (`sceneGeom.ts`) = `0.95` — the wireframe FILLS the sphere (outer extent at the visible edge) while staying contained (`1.0` protruded, `0.85` was too small). **Landed crispness (matches the #services centerpiece):** the SETTLED in-sphere wireframe reads as FINE crisp dots (like the centerpiece + the sphere's own dotted shell), NOT fat beads. Two `wireCrisp`-gated shader moves in `BrandmarkPhysicsCore/shaders.ts` make the landed state adopt the centerpiece's recipe: (1) dot size `sizeMul *= mix(1.0, uCleanFieldDotScale, wireCrisp * (1.0 - uCleanField))` — landed dots shrink to the same `~0.5×` fine speck the centerpiece uses (the `(1 - uCleanField)` factor keeps the centerpiece byte-identical — it already applied `uCleanFieldDotScale` at line 302, so this must be a no-op there); (2) density `depthKeep = mix(depthKeep, uCleanFieldKeep, wireCrisp)` — landed draw lifts to the centerpiece's `~0.65` keep so the fine dots form continuous lines. Both are `≈0` mid-flight (wireCrisp = `smoothstep(0.7,1.0,uDepth)`), so the FLIGHT is byte-identical. (An earlier de-pixelate pass went the wrong way — bigger dots `mix(1,0.90)` + `CORRIDOR_DRAW_TARGET 2600`; the fat dots read chunky. `CORRIDOR_DRAW_TARGET` now only affects the flight; the landed density comes from the wireCrisp lift.) Pitfalls: dropping the `(1 - uCleanField)` factor on the size line (centerpiece double-shrinks to 0.25); raising the global particle count past 6000 to densify (jumps the sim texture 64×64→128×128 = 4× compute); changing the corridor flat color without the SVG fill (handoff pops); raising `BRANDMARK_SPHERE_FILL` toward 1.0 (protrusion returns).

**Elegant SVG→3D transition (no flat-grainy dwell):** the particles can't read as a crisp SVG at corridor density (solid fill needs big dots → grainy), so the fix is to spend MINIMAL time in the flat handoff stage. `BRANDMARK_CORE_DEPTH_START_BLEND` = `0.0` (was `0.15`) and the `depth` ramp in `BrandmarkPhysicsCoreActor` is a FRONT-LOADED ease-out (`1-(1-t)^2`, was `smootherstep` which was flat at both ends): `uDepth` is still `0` at the exact swap frame (matched-pixel handoff stays seamless) but rises promptly after, so the mark peels into the luminous 3D fly-in right away instead of dwelling as a flat grainy crosshair. It still reaches `uDepth=1` with zero slope at the wrap peak (`t=1`) so the wireframe settles smoothly, in sync with the substrate sphere. Pitfall: reverting to `smootherstep` or raising `DEPTH_START_BLEND` reintroduces the flat dwell; making the flat handoff dots FINER makes the grainy fill worse, not better (it wants big dots to read solid — the dwell-time fix is the right lever).

**Card ring (2026-07-10, ADR-029).** With `SERVICES_CARD_RING` on (same module as the unification flag), `CorridorArmillary` also mounts `ServicesCardRing` — the four service photo cards as textured planes orbiting the parked mark INSIDE `pointerLookRef` (one instrument). Rotation is scroll-owned (`useServicesStageScroll` → `lib/services-ring/ringProgressRef`, math in `lib/services-ring/ringMath.ts`, unit-pinned) through a hard-capped underdamped spring — **never add a wall-clock rotation term** (ADR-021 addendum). The per-service rig pose (`getServicePose`) is PARKED AT FRONTAL under this flag — the ring's quarter-turns are the per-service turn; restoring the pose yaw double-rotates the cards off front-center. Cards: normal blending, opaque-void chamfer texels, depthWrite hysteresis-gated to the near-front card only (particles are depthWrite:false — an always-writing translucent card punches holes in the mark). **Update 1 (same day):** the centerpiece Lissajous drift is GATED OFF under `SERVICES_CARD_RING` (at the drift multiply in `BrandmarkPhysicsCoreActor` — flag-off byte-identical): at park the instrument moves ONLY from pointer-look and scroll. Each card is a transparent DEVICE SLAB (per-card group: glow −0.1 / extruded chamfered glass slab 0 / EdgesGeometry glint 0.05 / baked content plane 0.1 — all renderOrder < 1, i.e. below the mark's point pass; glass NEVER writes depth) riding ITS OWN orbit (`placeCardOnOrbit`, a = π/2 − φ on the HologramOrbits ellipse parametrization; nz stays PARAMETRIC cos φ) with a drawn track per card (`cardTrackOrbits.ts`, non-ServiceId ids). Pitfalls: drifting the ring's mount media query apart from the services DOM gate (tablets end up cardless); reintroducing pose yaw OR the parked drift with the flag on; additive blending on the cards; letting glass/glint/glow write depth; publishing a hit anchor for the opposite-of-front card.

**In-canvas cases card in the gyro assembly (ADR-036, supersedes the ADR-035 DOM overlay + ADR-033 orbit + ADR-034 terrace).** The four production cases render as ONE in-canvas 3D portrait tools card (`ArcCasesCard`, the ADR-029/033 device-slab grammar) mounted as a SIBLING of `ShellStack` inside the `gyroAssembly` group (`BrandmarkAccretionShell`) — NOT inside `pointerLookRef` (that group still carries ONLY the services card ring). The card shares the stream groups' shell-local space and banks with them, so the screen reads as physically MOUNTED on the nodes: while armed the sources/surfaces DOM labels fade on `arcCasesLevelRef.level` (`gateStackLabel`), and `ShellStack` FOLDS the source/surface node streams onto the card's actual left/right slab side walls using the same ref, which now carries the card's slab edges (`cardEdges`, shell-local) instead of a screen `panelRect`. The single `level` writer is `ArcCasesCard`'s `useFrame` at priority −5 (before ShellStack folds at 0); the fold is DIRECT shell-local math (no viewport unprojection, no per-frame camera re-solve — both retired with the overlay) and a per-frame morph of the existing stream geometry, flag-off / unarmed = rest pose byte-identical. The corridor camera stays a pure Z dolly (no camera channel — retired with ADR-034). The `arcBandFactor` epilogue-kill `[0, 0.1]` keeps the cases reveal provably disjoint from the services ring (`epilogueProgress ≥ 0.72`) — never weaken either clock. Pitfalls: re-introducing the DOM overlay panel or its `panelRect` unprojection latch (rejected twice); mounting the card in `pointerLookRef` or world-fixed instead of the gyro assembly (it must bank WITH the streams); a lateral/vertical camera channel; drifting the `ARC_CASES_MEDIA` gate from the station-headers hide media (gate parity).
