**Arc Cases: the reveal is PHASED (ADR-041); the trigger is a cue under the Build title (ADR-042).** Still no scroll writer and no new clock — the ONE damped arm level is split into two ordered phases: the node fold runs on `arcFoldInput(level)` (done at 0.62) and the CARD on `arcCardPresence(level)` (`smootherstep(0.62, 1)`, published as `cardPresence`), so the nodes fold and latch BEFORE the screen materializes into the frame they made. The trigger is `ArcCasesCue` — a DOM dotted-leader + label docked under the Build station title (NOT the retired ADR-041 sphere sigil; its `intelligence.sigil` anchor, `gateSigil`, and `SIGIL_Z` are gone) — which arms only once the sources/surfaces notes have SETTLED (`sigilSettle` on the smoothed stack reveal) and owns the auto-disarm watcher. Do NOT retune `ARC_BAND_IN` to chase the stack window — the park sits below the accretion peak, so raising it gates the card off; the sequencing is enforced on the trigger.

**Arc Cases: no camera channel, no scroll writer (ADR-036, supersedes ADR-035).** The click-owned cases reveal adds NO scroll writer AND NO camera channel — the corridor camera is a pure Z dolly (X = 0 end-to-end). The reveal is ONE in-canvas 3D tools card (`ArcCasesCard`) mounted in the gyro assembly; on arm the sources/surfaces DOM labels fade via `arcCasesLevelRef` (single writer = the card's R3F `useFrame` at priority −5, which also carries the card's slab `cardEdges` — shell-local, NOT a screen `panelRect`). Readers: `gateStackLabel` (label opacity), the caption fade, the DOM stepper, and `ShellStack` in the R3F tree, which folds the source/surface node streams onto the card's slab side walls by DIRECT shell-local math (no unprojection, no per-frame camera re-solve; flag-off / unarmed / reduced-motion = rest pose byte-identical). It only GATES on `paintProgress`/`epilogueProgress` (`arcBandFactor`). Do NOT re-introduce a lateral/vertical camera channel, a scroll writer, the DOM overlay, or the `panelRect` latch.

**Corridor-exit next station is `#practice` (ADR-049; was `#continuum` under ADR-047, `#about` under ADR-033).** `useCorridorExitScroll` resolves `nextStation = CONTINUUM_RAIL_STAGE ? (#practice ?? #contact) : ABOUT_DECK_STAGE ? (#continuum ?? #practice) : (#about ?? #continuum)` — BOTH `#about` (deck-flip stage) and `#continuum` (rail stage) are pinned TRANSPARENT stages the ambient hold must SURVIVE, and `#practice` is now the opaque cover that ends it. The ambient bottom gate is keyed to the SAME `nextStationTopVh` the fade envelope reads — a rect-boundary conjunction hard-cuts the canvas at exactly the next station's top (the ADR-030 Update 1 §6 seam bug, recorded twice). `NEXT_STATION_FADE_START_VH = 0.6 / END_VH = 0.0` keep their values, now keyed to `#practice.top`. `#continuum`'s stage writes come only from `useContinuumStageScroll` (single writer: `data-continuum-mode`, `--continuum-approach/copy-in/rail-form/bg-in`, the `data-continuum-formed` reticle-launch hysteresis, `continuumStageProgressRef`); every disengage clears the attribute (fail-static → the static `.crail` 7s loop). The continuum beat (mark lift to ~0.60 ink + half recede release) is a pure function of `continuumFormT(aboutP, continuumP)` (ADR-049 Update 2 — the mark re-inks DURING the `#about` exit slide via the `CONTINUUM_FORM_PRELUDE` term, then the continuum approach carries it home) — no new scroll writer beyond the stage hook. **The continuum carries NO orbit emphasis (ADR-049 Update 3): the waist-ring re-brighten is REMOVED — the structural rings stay cleared through #about/#continuum on the plain exit getter; do not re-add a per-ring continuum override.** The spectrum is the crail INSTRUMENT, formed FROM the mark (ADR-049 Update 4): the dashed rail + bearings + register brackets wipe outward from the mark's centre on the scrubbed `--continuum-rail-form` envelope (a clip-path inset release — never scaleX; stops/readout/CTA bloom centre-out on per-child `--cs-off`, the masthead alone stays on `--continuum-copy-in`), and the reticle is the ONE discrete channel — `data-continuum-formed` (hysteresis SET `CONTINUUM_RETICLE_LAUNCH_AT` 0.98 / CLEAR `CONTINUUM_RETICLE_RESET_BELOW` 0.88, opacity scrubbed to 0 at both edges so the flip is invisible) starts its centre → Tool launch + the fallback crail's TRUE 7s round-trip ping-pong (0/100% Tool, 50% Collaborator — not `infinite alternate`). The Update-3 mark-band shader block (`uBand*` in `volumetricShaders.ts`, envelopes in `continuumBandMath.ts`, lab `/test/continuum-band`) was REJECTED for production and stays 0-gain look-dev; ADR-049 Update 1's bold DOM axis and the edge-on 3D `ContinuumWaistRail` thumb are both retired (the Update-2 waist-ring beam layer was deleted the same day it was built, never live). The stage hook now engages via a bounded settle loop (400ms, ≤ 20 tries) so it never stays fail-static waiting on a post-corridor-mount scroll frame. The `#about` lead-in EXIT is a SLIDE-OUT (ADR-047 Update 8): the copy slides left + the portrait right on `--about-exit` over the live corridor bed — NO fade-to-shield. `--about-bg-in` is written 0 ONCE at engage and stays there for the engaged life (restores to opaque only via the disengage var-clear → fail-opaque default 1); the lockstep cover before the `#practice` ambient kill is now `#continuum`'s `--continuum-bg-in` tail. `#about`'s stage writes come only from `useAboutStageScroll` (single writer: `data-about-mode`, `--about-flip/shift/copy-in/exit`, `--about-bg-in` (once at engage), `aboutStageProgressRef`, `aboutSlotRef`); every disengage clears the attribute (fail-static → the `.voidwalker` fallback). The Build-park cases reveal (ADR-036) adds NO scroll writer and NO camera effect — it is click-owned and only GATES on `paintProgress`/`epilogueProgress`; it is an in-canvas 3D card, and the corridor camera stays a pure Z dolly through arm/disarm.

**Home-v2 corridor-exit invariant (ADR-021):** the production seam after the corridor epilogue is a **zoom-dissipate** — the docked R3F canvas plays a fly-into-sphere arc + particle scatter while the brandmark RIDES the sphere off-screen and then re-centres into the (now empty) Services section. `useCorridorExitScroll` owns the dissipate clock: raw progress is stretched over ~2 viewports (post 2026-06-16, ADR-021 follow-up; was 1.58), then eased into `--corridor-dissipate` / `dockProgress`. The hook ALSO writes a `data-services-brandmark` gate (`"hold"` then `"fade"`) + `--services-brandmark` (0..1) opacity var once the dock releases — `ProjectedBrandmarkActor` reads the gate to release the brandmark to CSS, which holds it fixed-centred in `#services` then fades it as `#continuum.top` crosses the `0.5 → 0.1 vh` band. The per-element reveal channels (`--services-header-in`, `--services-grid-in`, `--services-cta-in`) are GONE — `#services` was stripped of its header/cards/CTA in the same revision; restore those channels only if those elements come back. Keep the dock channel (`docked` / `dockProgress`) writes scoped to one hook; do not write `epilogueProgress` / `paintProgress` from the exit hook. The retired cover-plane sweep recipe (lower opaque plane covers a held canvas, first-read copy lives inside the cover) is preserved in `components/landing/home-v2/handoff-lab/` + `/test/handoff-a|b|c` and documented in ADR-021 — reuse that recipe verbatim if a later section needs an Active Theory / Hashgraph-class cover sweep; do not rebuild it.

**Seam pixel-field handoff (ADR-021 follow-up Phase 2, 2026-06-16):** on the capable path the SVG brandmark in `#services` is REPLACED by `CorridorSeamPixelField` — a fixed 2D canvas that paints the brandmark as a 3px gold/dawn pixel grid sampled from `BRANDMARK_FULL_PATHS` (same source the SVG glyph and `BrandmarkSilhouettePoints` use; ADR-014 single-source-of-truth). The dissolve begins the MOMENT the welded mark re-centres and shows itself — which is during the dock tail (`markCentred = docked ? dissipate >= MARK_CENTRED_DOCK_PROGRESS (0.85) : rawDissipate >= 0.999`), NOT only after the dock releases — and runs a long runway driven by `#continuum`'s approach (`seamMorph = smoothstep01((SEAM_MORPH_START_VH 1.9 - continuum.top/vh) / (1.9 - SEAM_MORPH_END_VH 0.1))`). The hook publishes `transform.seamMorph` + `--services-pixelate` + the scoping `data-services-pixelate="true"` attribute whenever `dockCapable && sectionNearDock && markCentred && continuum.top/vh < SEAM_MORPH_START_VH`; it may legitimately co-exist with `data-corridor-docked` (that is the whole point — the morph opens before dock release). The pixel field reads `seamMorph` from the store; `home-v2.css` overrides the SVG glyph to `opacity: 0` and shows the canvas under `data-services-pixelate`. The actor-lifecycle gate (`data-services-brandmark` hold/fade + `--services-brandmark` opacity) is preserved unchanged for the SVG release + the fallback path. Square-pixel aesthetic is an INTENTIONAL borrow from `/test/gateway` (`ImageParticleGateway`) and an explicit ADR-015 exception — the seam field is OUTSIDE the brandmark painter cap (its own 2D canvas, its own rAF, its own brand-journey clock), so the cap (atmosphere + silhouette globals + substrate-sphere intelligence-layer mesh) is unchanged. All dispersal math lives in `lib/home-v2/seamPixelize.ts` and is unit-tested; do not duplicate the easing or the grid-snap inside the canvas component.

**Hero → Thoughtform seam invariant (ADR-022 v8):** the hero → corridor seam is a **ToyFight curtain reveal** — the **hero is the moving layer** (`.hero`, `position: relative; z-index: 4`) that scrolls up and off the first viewport, uncovering the corridor's parked `ThoughtformCompassGate` frame held **frozen** behind it (`.home-corridor-host` z:3). NOT the inverse (v7's mistake: a held hero with the corridor rising to cover it), and NEVER a proxy/copy of the second section (v6's mistake). The freeze: `useLandingScroll` toggles `html[data-corridor-entry]` while the corridor stage top is below the viewport top, and `home-v2.css` makes `.home-v2-stage__sticky` `position: fixed; inset: 0` under that flag. At the handoff the flag clears and native sticky resumes at the same `top: 0`, with no transform on the sticky cell and never on `.home-v2-stage`. The hero may receive only a tiny capped inertial `translateY` correction for ToyFight-like smoothness; document scroll, corridor `progress` / `paintProgress` / `epilogueProgress` / `dockProgress`, and the hero video opacity remain untouched. Hero video stays opaque, never scaled/faded (ADR-008 Rule 3, gateway shield). ADR-022 history documents every superseded approach (v6 proxy sweep, v7 held-hero parallax/counter-translate) and its artifact.
