## The finding

**The control is the recommendation now.** Wave 01's `KA` was the shipped panel
and it FAILED its own gates in the grader's read; `KJ` was the argument against
it. Stage 1 made `KJ` the thing `mount=shipped` mounts, so `v0` and `KA` come
back byte-identical to each other and carry the composite's numbers — 200 gold
objects to 9, 27 % bold to none, the eleven rungs collapsed onto a base the HUD
frame already used.

That is also why the six committed control stills MOVED in this commit. They are
the baseline, and the baseline is supposed to be stable — but the baseline IS
`mount=shipped`, and the site moved under it deliberately. A control that did not
move here would mean stage 1 had not landed.
