# The subjects

⚠ On this ship a subject is a THEME, and its identity is a REGISTER STRIP —
three reference first screens stacked — never a photograph and never a page of
this site. The strip is what `qa.py` attaches as image 2 and what the gallery
shows beside every candidate; it proves how a ruled page seats, heads, labels,
frames and letters, and it proves nothing about this site's ground, layout, type
or copy. The rubric's `## Grading rules` say so verbatim, because the harness's
own preamble would otherwise call every difference a fault.

Not `dark` / `light`: `config.nouns()` scrubs subject keys as words from every
harvest note, and those two words are in every sentence about a theme.

## void — the dark theme

- **What it is:** dawn ink on the void, the site's default; every alpha in the
  sheet is a step off `--dawn-rgb` over `--void-rgb`.
- **Identity truth:** `references/register/void.png`, 1440 × 2700 — three
  first screens at 1440 × 900 stacked top to bottom:
  1. **Lighthouse** — the reference the owner named for clean sections and
     cards; hairline-ruled sections, cards with no radius and no shadow, one
     hue of line.
  2. **Tensorlake** — 147 rules on one page in one hue (read 2026-09-05,
     `DRIVE.md`); the page draws its own grid and every panel is seated on it.
  3. **Prime Intellect** — 150 rules, 15 accent objects on a whole page
     (2026-09-05); ordinals as wayfinding, cells sharing edges.
- **What the strip proves:** the ruled sheet (rules, seams, seated cells), the
  head band (kicker, ordinal, hairline), the accent budget, the two-face type.
- **What it does not prove:** any ground, any layout, any composition of THIS
  page. A candidate is not judged for looking unlike it.
- **Readings:** `references/register/readings/<site>.json`, one per source,
  from the capture's `--register`: rule count, hue count, radius count, faces,
  accent count. The numbers are the record; the strip is the picture.

## parchment — the light theme

- **What it is:** void ink on parchment; ADR-058 swaps `--dawn-rgb` and
  `--void-rgb` and the sheet re-derives every alpha in `theme.css` rather
  than inheriting the dark ones.
- **Identity truth:** `references/register/parchment.png`, 1440 × 2700:
  1. **Hermeus `/propulsion`** — the second section the owner named: the title
     in the left columns, the paragraph opposite, `02 / Roadmap` heads over a
     hairline, boxes seated on a year axis with the current one lit.
  2. **stripe.dev** — `[B] BLOG` mono nav, `/ SECTION` labels, `[ FIG. 1 ]`
     frames, a `/ METADATA` column, related posts two-up on a dashed divider.
  3. **Kindled + Kindred, "Grid System"** — a light page whose grid is drawn:
     rules at the band edges, cells sharing edges, chrome at the extremes.
- **What the strip proves:** the same grammar on a light ground — rules at a
  legible alpha on paper, ink not gold for small text, figures framed with a
  caption bar, an index as a ruled table.
- **What it does not prove:** as above.
